Who We Are

India's Trusted Partner for Survey & Geospatial Solutions

CSTech Survey & Co. was established to deliver a comprehensive range of consulting services backed by the latest survey instruments and deep domain expertise — empowering professionals across hydraulics, GIS & remote sensing, precision agriculture, transportation planning, and structural analysis.
