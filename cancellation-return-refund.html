WEBSITE LEGAL POLICY

CANCELLATION, RETURN, REFUND & WARRANTY POLICY

CSTECH SURVEY & CO.

Effective Date: 26 November 2026

01. Order Cancellation

  • Customers may request cancellation within 24 hours of order confirmation, provided the order has not been dispatched or committed to a non-cancellable special order.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible. Customers should contact our support team for assistance.
  • Bulk orders, customized products and special-order equipment may be subject to specific cancellation terms communicated before order confirmation.
  • Cancellation requests must be submitted through our official customer support email or phone number.
  • Any approved cancellation and applicable refund will be processed in accordance with the agreed order terms and applicable laws.
  • No cancellation charge will be imposed contrary to applicable consumer protection requirements.

02. Return Policy

Due to the specialized nature and high value of surveying instruments, returns are subject to verification and approval.

  • Change-of-mind returns may not be accepted for specially ordered, customized or specification-based equipment, subject to applicable consumer rights.
  • Products that are defective, damaged during transit, incorrect, incomplete or materially different from their agreed description may be eligible for repair, replacement, return or refund.
  • Customers should retain the original packaging, accessories, manuals, warranty documents and purchase invoice.
  • Customers must obtain a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) from CSTECH SURVEY & CO. before returning a product.
  • Products must be returned in a condition that permits proper inspection, except where damage or defects make this impossible.
  • Return requests will be evaluated individually based on the nature of the issue and applicable law.

03. Defective Products & RMA Process

In case of manufacturing defects, missing items or transit damage, customers should contact our support team as soon as reasonably possible, preferably within 2–5 days of delivery.

An RMA request should include:

  • Order number and invoice details.
  • Product name, model and serial number.
  • Description of the reported issue.
  • Supporting photographs or videos, wherever applicable.
  • Photographs of the outer packaging and damaged product in case of transit damage.

Additional Conditions

  • Customers should retain the original packaging until the issue has been assessed.
  • Products approved for RMA should be returned with the original accessories and supplied documents, wherever applicable.
  • Returned products may undergo technical inspection and verification.
  • Following inspection, eligible products may be repaired, replaced or refunded, depending on the nature of the issue, product availability, applicable warranty terms and consumer protection laws.
  • Repair or replacement may be offered as an appropriate resolution where legally permissible.

04. Refund Policy

Refunds may be approved in the following circumstances:

  • A product is confirmed to have an eligible defect that cannot reasonably be rectified.
  • A replacement is unavailable or unsuitable as a lawful resolution.
  • An order is successfully cancelled before dispatch.
  • A refund is otherwise required under applicable law or the agreed terms of sale.

Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds will generally be initiated within 7–14 business days after approval and completion of the required verification.
  • Online payment refunds will normally be processed through the original payment method.
  • Actual credit timelines may depend on the bank, card issuer or payment service provider.
  • Shipping, handling and installation charges will be assessed according to the reason for the return and applicable law. Such charges will not automatically be treated as non-refundable.
  • Customers will be informed if additional information or documentation is required.

05. Warranty Policy

Warranty coverage is subject to the applicable manufacturer's or supplier's written warranty terms for the particular product.

  • Warranty duration and coverage may vary according to brand, model and product category.
  • The applicable warranty period will be communicated at the time of purchase or specified in the warranty documentation.
  • Customers must retain the original purchase invoice and product serial number for warranty claims.
  • Warranty claims may be declined where the reported damage is attributable to misuse, unauthorized repairs, physical damage, improper installation, negligence or normal wear and tear, subject to the actual warranty terms and applicable law.
  • Battery, charger, cables, accessories and consumable components may have separate warranty conditions.
  • CSTECH SURVEY & CO. will assist customers in coordinating eligible warranty claims with the relevant manufacturer or supplier.
  • Warranty service will be provided in accordance with the applicable warranty terms.

06. Product Inspection & Verification

To maintain transparency and protect both customers and the company, CSTECH SURVEY & CO. may maintain product inspection records.

These may include:

  • Product model and serial number verification.
  • Pre-dispatch functional testing, wherever applicable.
  • Verification of supplied accessories and components.
  • Product condition and packaging records.
  • Inspection reports for products received under an approved RMA request.

The outcome of an inspection will be communicated to the customer. Inspection findings will be considered alongside the applicable warranty terms and consumer protection laws.

07. Delivery & Transit Damage

Customers are encouraged to inspect the delivered package and equipment upon receipt.

  • Any visible damage to the package or equipment should be documented through photographs or videos.
  • Transit damage, missing accessories or incorrect deliveries should be reported to CSTECH SURVEY & CO. as soon as reasonably possible, preferably within 2–5 days of delivery.
  • Customers should retain the original packaging and shipping materials until the matter is resolved.
  • Delivery-related complaints will be reviewed with the relevant logistics or courier partner, wherever necessary.
  • Reporting periods are intended to facilitate timely investigation and will not override any rights available under applicable law.

08. Final Inspection & Resolution

  • Products returned under an approved RMA request may be inspected by our technical team or the authorized manufacturer/service centre.
  • The final findings and proposed resolution will be communicated to the customer after verification.
  • Any repair, replacement or refund will be handled according to the applicable product warranty, agreed terms of sale and consumer protection laws.
  • Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or limit any statutory rights or remedies available to customers under applicable law.
