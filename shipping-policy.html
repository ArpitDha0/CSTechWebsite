SHIPPING POLICY

CSTECH SURVEY & CO.

Effective Date: 26 November 2026

01. Introduction

CSTECH Survey & Co. is committed to delivering surveying instruments, geospatial equipment and related accessories to customers in a reliable and professional manner.

This Shipping Policy outlines the general terms and conditions applicable to the shipment and delivery of products purchased or ordered through our company.

02. Order Processing

Orders are processed after confirmation of product availability, order details and applicable payment terms.

  • Orders are subject to stock availability.
  • Additional verification may be required before dispatch.
  • Processing time may vary depending on the product.
  • Customers will be informed about dispatch details wherever applicable.

03. Shipping & Delivery

Products may be shipped through third-party courier services, transport companies or other suitable logistics providers.

Delivery timelines are estimated and may vary depending on the customer's location, shipping method, stock availability, transportation conditions and other circumstances.

04. Delivery Charges

Shipping and transportation charges, where applicable, will be communicated to the customer during quotation, order confirmation or before dispatch.

Delivery charges may depend on product weight, dimensions, destination and selected transportation method.

05. Shipping Address

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate shipping information, including:

  • Full name or company name
  • Complete delivery address
  • City, state and PIN code
  • Contact phone number
  • Any necessary delivery instructions

CSTECH Survey & Co. shall not be responsible for delivery delays or issues caused by incorrect or incomplete information provided by the customer.

06. Delays in Delivery

While we make reasonable efforts to ensure timely delivery, delays may occur due to logistics issues, weather conditions, transportation disruptions, remote locations, holidays, unforeseen events or circumstances beyond our control.

Such delays do not automatically qualify an order for cancellation or refund unless applicable law or the agreed order terms provide otherwise.

07. Shipment Tracking

Where tracking information is available from the logistics provider, it may be shared with the customer after dispatch.

Customers can use the provided tracking information to monitor the shipment status.

08. Inspection Upon Delivery

Customers are encouraged to inspect the shipment and packaging upon receipt.

  • Check the package for visible damage.
  • Verify the delivered products and accessories.
  • Report missing or damaged items promptly.
  • Keep the invoice, packaging and delivery documentation.

09. Damaged or Missing Products

If a product is received damaged, incomplete or incorrect, customers should contact CSTECH Survey & Co. as soon as reasonably possible with relevant photographs, order details and supporting documentation.

Reported issues will be reviewed and handled in accordance with the applicable company policy, manufacturer policy and consumer rights under Indian law.

10. International Shipping

International shipments, if offered, are subject to destination country regulations, customs requirements, import duties, taxes and other applicable charges.

Any applicable customs duties, import taxes or other charges will be communicated or handled as agreed for the relevant order.
