Surveying & Engineering 

APEKS land equipment provides high-precision and high-efficiency solutions for the surveying and mapping industry, including advanced equipment such as GNSS receivers, total stations, laser scanners, etc. Which are widely used in topographic surveying and mapping, engineering surveying, cadastral surveys and smart city construction, helping the surveying and mapping industry to achieve accurate and intelligent data collection and analysis.

3D Scanning Mapping

APEKS Handheld 3D scanners capture high-precision models (point clouds/meshes) for reverse engineering, inspections, and volume measurements. Key benefits include mobility, real-time scanning, and CAD compatibility. Ideal for industrial, construction, and cultural preservation applications. A cost-effective alternative to traditional surveying.

Monitoring

APEKS GNSS monitoring provides precise, real-time and long-term tracking of ground movements and structural deformations with millimeter accuracy. It supports landslide detection, tectonic studies, infrastructure safety, and environmental monitoring. GNSS data aids in weather forecasting, precision agriculture, and autonomous navigation by providing reliable positioning and environmental insights.
