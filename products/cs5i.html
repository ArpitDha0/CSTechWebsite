CS5i Rugged Surveying Controller Built for Total Station & GNSS Field Work

5" Sunlight-Readable Display

High-brightness 450-nit IPS touchscreen remains clearly visible under direct sunlight. Capacitive multi-touch with glove-mode and wet-finger operation keeps work going even in adverse field conditions.

Military-Grade Ruggedness

IP67 dust and water resistance combined with 1.5m drop protection ensures the CS5i performs reliably across construction sites, mining operations, and field survey work — from -30°C to +60°C.

Android 13 Field Platform

Octa-core processor with 4 GB RAM runs all leading survey software — SurPad, Hi-Survey, FieldGenius, and more. 64 GB internal storage expandable via microSD for unlimited data capacity.

Full Connectivity Suite

Dual SIM 4G LTE for NTRIP corrections, Bluetooth 5.0 for wireless total station control, WiFi 802.11ac, NFC, and USB-C OTG. Connects to all major GNSS receivers and total stations over Bluetooth.

Hot-Swap 6000mAh Battery

Removable and hot-swappable 6000 mAh battery enables continuous operation without powering down. Carry spare batteries for extended field sessions far from power sources.
