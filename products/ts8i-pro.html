TS8i Pro Rugged GNSS Tablet Sub-Centimeter RTK Accuracy in the Field

Built-in RTK GNSS Module

Integrated multi-band GNSS receiver supporting GPS L1/L2, GLONASS L1/L2, BeiDou B1/B2/B3, Galileo E1/E5 and RTK positioning with accuracy up to 1 cm + 1 ppm — no external receiver needed.

IP67 Rugged & Drop Resistant

Military-grade chassis rated IP67 for full dust and water immersion protection. Withstands 1.5m drops on concrete. Operates reliably between -30°C to +60°C for the harshest survey environments.

5G / 4G LTE Connectivity

Dual SIM 5G + 4G LTE with WiFi 802.11ac, Bluetooth 5.1, and NFC enables real-time NTRIP correction stream reception and cloud-based GIS data management without a separate modem.

8" Sunlight-Readable Touchscreen

Bright 500-nit IPS display with capacitive multi-touch and stylus support, readable in direct sunlight. 13MP autofocus camera with flash for geotagged field photography and documentation.

All-Day 9000mAh Battery

Large-capacity battery with fast charging keeps the TS8i Pro running a full 10-hour field shift. Android 13 with Qualcomm octa-core processor runs all major surveying software natively.
