TCA930 Choke Ring GNSS Antenna Full-Band Multi-Constellation Geodetic Antenna

Full-Band Multi-Constellation

Tracks GPS L1/L2/L5/L-Band, GLONASS L1/L2/L3, Galileo E1/E5a/E5b/E6, BDS B1/B2/B3, QZSS, SBAS, NavIC L5. Axial ratio ≤2dB ensures excellent circular polarization.

Superior Multipath Rejection

Patented choke ring design with excellent out-of-band rejection suppresses EMI in dense wireless communication environments. Reliable in obstructed survey environments.

Key Features

Full L-Band Coverage

Complete tracking of all GNSS L-band signals including GPS L5, Galileo E6, GLONASS L3 and BDS B3 for maximum positioning redundancy.

50dB LNA Gain

High-gain low-noise amplifier with 50dB typical gain and noise figure ≤2dB delivers clean signal to the connected receiver in all conditions.

IP67 Rated

Fully sealed to IP67 for permanent outdoor CORS station and structural monitoring installations exposed to rain and humidity.

MTBF >30,000 hrs

Industrial-grade reliability with mean time between failures exceeding 30,000 hours for unattended continuous reference station operation.
