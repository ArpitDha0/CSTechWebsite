TS8i 5G Rugged Tablet Field-Ready. 5G-Connected. Built Tough.

5G Ultra-Fast Connectivity

Dual SIM 5G and 4G LTE with WiFi 802.11ac enables real-time GIS data uploads and seamless cloud integration even in remote surveying locations.

Military-Grade Ruggedness

Qualcomm Octa-Core Performance
