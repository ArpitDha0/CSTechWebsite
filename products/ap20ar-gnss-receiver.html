AR stakeout

Integrates a wide angel camera that seamlessly switches with the handheld controller, overlaying design guidance onto the live scene. Simply follow the on screen arrows in real time to walk directly to each point with clarity and precision-enabling faster, more intuitive stakeout.

Rugged and Durable Design

IP68 and Iko8 ratings provide dust, water, shock, and vibration resistance. The alloy body ensures durability and reliable performance in tough field conditions, allowing you to survey with confidence even in harsh environments.

OLED Color Display

The upgraded 0.96" OLED color screen that provides clear, easy-to-read information at a glance. DualLED physical buttons allow quick switching between modes and data link control, no need controller. This intuitive interface enhances user experience and ensures efficient operation even in challenging field conditions.

2W UHF Rx/Tx radio

Featuring a built-in integrated radio transceiver with an 8-15km range, AP20 matches the performance of large external radios. Capable of func- tioning as both a base and rover, it also operates efficiently as an NTRIP rover when connected to a GNSS RTK network.

Up to 120° IMU

Equipped with a calibration-free 4.0 IMU sensor, AP20 delivers accurate, interference-free orientation without the need for calibration or initialization. Its tilt Capability of up to120° enhances measurement efficiency by up to 50%， making it ideal for complex surveying environments.
