Leica FlexLine TS03 3'' & 5'' Angular Accuracy | Reflectorless 500m | Lightweight

3'' / 5'' Accuracy

Available in 3'' and 5'' angular accuracy variants, ideal for standard construction and civil engineering surveys.

Reflectorless 500m

PinPoint EDM technology measures up to 500m without a prism, enabling safe measurement of inaccessible points.

Dual-Face Display

Large graphic display on both faces with backlit keyboard for convenient operation in any lighting condition.

Lightweight Design

Compact and lightweight at just 4.9kg, making it easy to carry and set up in the field all day long.

30-Hour Battery

IP55 Protection

Dust and water protected to IP55 standard, ensuring reliable performance in challenging field environments.
