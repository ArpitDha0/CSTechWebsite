Topcon AT-B4 Auto Level 28× Magnification | ±2.0mm/km | Reliable & Affordable

28× Magnification

28× telescope magnification provides clear, detailed staff readings suitable for general construction and leveling survey work.

±2.0mm/km Accuracy

Standard deviation of ±2.0mm per kilometre of double-run leveling, well-suited for construction site layout and floor leveling.

Automatic Compensator

Pendulum-type automatic compensator for reliable horizontal line of sight setup without manual adjustment of the line of sight.

Quick Setup

Simple three-screw leveling base and large circular bubble ensure fast instrument setup, minimising downtime on busy job sites.

Compact & Portable

Lightweight and compact body makes the AT-B4 easy to carry and set up repeatedly across large construction projects.

Topcon Reliability

Topcon's proven build quality delivers consistent, dependable performance across years of regular field use in varied conditions.
