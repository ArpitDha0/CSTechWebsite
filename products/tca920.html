TCA920 Choke Ring GNSS Antenna All-Constellation Geodetic Reference Station Antenna

All-Signal GNSS Reception

Supports all-correlated signals of four GNSS systems: GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo, QZSS, SBAS, NavIC. High antenna gain with effective satellite reception at low elevation angles.

Stable Phase Center Accuracy

Patented multi-point feeding technology with stable phase center ensures sub-millimeter positioning accuracy. Excellent multipath reduction across all GNSS frequency bands.

Key Features

IP67 Protection

Fully sealed to IP67 standard — dustproof and waterproof for permanent outdoor reference station installations.

-40°C to +85°C

Operates reliably across extreme temperature ranges, from arctic cold to desert heat without performance degradation.

TNC Female Connector

Standard TNC female RF connector with 50Ω nominal impedance for direct connection to GNSS receivers and splitters.

MTBF >30,000 hrs

High reliability design with mean time between failures exceeding 30,000 hours for continuous CORS network deployment.
