CS5i Pro GNSS RTK Controller Professional Android Data Collector with Integrated RTK

Purpose-Built for Field Surveying

The CS5i Pro combines a high-performance Android controller with built-in GNSS RTK positioning engine, hot-swappable battery, and a 5-inch sunlight-readable display — everything a surveyor needs in a single rugged handheld device.

Key Features

Multi-Band RTK GNSS

GPS, GLONASS, BeiDou, Galileo multi-band with RTK accuracy of 1 cm horizontal, 2 cm vertical.

IP67 Rugged Protection

Fully sealed against dust and water. 1.5m drop resistance. Operates from -30°C to +60°C.

Android 13 Platform

Runs all major field survey software. Octa-core processor with 4 GB RAM for smooth performance.

5" Sunlight Display

720×1280 IPS screen at 450 nits brightness. Clearly readable under direct sunlight in the field.

Hot-Swap Battery

6000 mAh removable battery with hot-swap support for uninterrupted operation across full-day field shifts.

4G LTE + WiFi

Dual SIM 4G LTE, WiFi 802.11ac, Bluetooth 5.0 and NFC for NTRIP corrections and data synchronisation.
